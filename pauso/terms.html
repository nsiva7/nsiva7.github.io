1. The app

Pauso is a focus tool. It shows a pause screen before apps you choose, and can block those apps during schedules, after a daily time limit, or while strict mode is on. Pauso contains no ads. Features may change, be added or be removed over time.

2. What Pauso can and cannot do

  • Pauso works only while its accessibility service is enabled and running. Some phones stop background services to save battery; the app explains how to prevent this, but we cannot guarantee it on every device.
  • Blocking is a tool to help you, not a security control. A determined user can bypass it, for example by restarting the phone in safe mode. Do not rely on Pauso to restrict another person's device.
  • Strict mode locks Pauso's own settings, the system accessibility page and the uninstall dialog until the time you chose. There is no early exit. You choose the length; by starting strict mode you accept that it runs to the end.

3. Pauso Premium

  • What you get: pausing more than three apps, block schedules, daily limits and strict mode.
  • Plans: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, sold through Google Play. The price for your country is shown in the app and in Google Play before you confirm.
  • Free trial: a plan may include a free trial for people who have not had Premium before. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the plan's price is charged when it ends.
  • Renewal: subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period at the then-current price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. If a price changes, Google Play will notify you in advance as required.
  • Cancelling: cancel any time in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • When Premium ends: the pause screen keeps working for your first three paused apps. Schedules, daily limits and strict mode stop applying but your settings are kept.
  • Refunds: payments are handled by Google Play and follow Google Play's refund policy. For other problems, contact us.
  • Restoring: Premium is tied to your Google account. Use Restore purchases on the Pauso Premium screen on a new device signed in with the same account.
