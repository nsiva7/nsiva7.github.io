Features

36 themes

A newspaper, a pixel game, Swiss tiles, an ocean, an aquarium, a garden and more. Each one is a whole new home.

Live effects

Rain, snow, falling leaves and petals, a starry night sky, touch ripples, particles and a charging glow on any theme.

Widgets that fit

Clock, weather, calendar, alarms, steps, screen time, now playing and a focus timer, styled to match your theme.

Make it yours

Choose your home apps, icons, wallpaper, app drawer, folders and effects for each theme.

For everyone

Clarity's big, clear buttons with an SOS shortcut, and Playground, a simple home a parent can set up for kids.

14 languages

Follows your phone's language automatically, or pick one in More settings.
