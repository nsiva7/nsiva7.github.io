FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I make UniUI my home screen?

Open UniUI and press Home, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app and choose UniUI. You can switch back any time.

Will switching themes lose my setup?

No. Each theme remembers its own home apps and settings, so you can switch freely and come back.

Why does UniUI ask for accessibility access?

Only for double-tap to lock the screen. UniUI does not read your screen or what you type. It's optional.

Which permissions does it need?

Only what a widget you use needs: calendar for events, approximate location for weather, usage access for screen time, Health Connect for steps, and notification access for now playing. Read the Privacy Policy.

Does Playground lock my child in?

Playground shows only the apps you allow, with a daily time limit. Settings are behind a 4-digit parent PIN, with a lockout after too many wrong tries.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.1 and newer. Double-tap to lock needs Android 9 or newer.
