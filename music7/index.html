Features

Your library, offline

Songs, albums, artists, genres, folders, playlists and favourites from your phone.

Presets, custom bands and 15 visualizer styles, full screen or behind the art.

Discover

Jellyfin

Stream your own Jellyfin music server alongside the songs on your phone.
