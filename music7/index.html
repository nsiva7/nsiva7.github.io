Features

Everything your music needs

Your songs, your server and the world's radio, in one beautiful player.

Colours from your music

Let the whole app take its colours from the album art of the song that's playing, from your wallpaper (Material You, Android 12+), or from the theme itself.

  • Three colour sources: theme, album art or wallpaper
  • Day, Night or Auto mode
  • Blurred album-art background with adjustable strength

Your library, organised

Plays the music on your phone, offline. Browse it your way with up to 11 tabs you can show or hide.

  • Songs, Albums, Artists, Genres, Folders, Playlists and Favourites
  • Recently added and recently played
  • Search your library and online music in one place
  • Play next, add to queue, playlists, song info and share
  • Hide short clips and ringtones, and remember where you left off

Stream your Jellyfin server

Sign in to your own Jellyfin server and play your whole collection anywhere, right next to the songs on your phone.

  • Choose which server music folders to use
  • "Combine with my music" to mix server and phone songs
  • Browse, edit and create playlists on the server
  • Your login token is stored encrypted; your password is never saved

Shape your sound

The equalizer uses your phone's audio engine. Pick a preset or move each band yourself; your custom setting is saved and applied to everything you play.

  • Presets from your phone, plus your own custom bands
  • Turn it on or off in one tap

15 live visualizers

Watch your music come alive, full screen, in portrait or landscape, with or without the album art behind it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Music 7 free?

Yes. Music 7 is free with ads. Premium removes ads and unlocks the hero player themes.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your songs play offline. Discover (charts, Audius and radio) and Jellyfin need an internet connection.

Which audio formats does it play?

MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV and the other formats your phone supports.

What do I need for Jellyfin?

Your own Jellyfin server address and account. Music 7 stores only an encrypted access token, never your password.

What data does Music 7 collect?

No accounts and no analytics. Ads in the free version are served by Google, and you can change your ad privacy choices in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer. Colours from your wallpaper need Android 12 or newer.
